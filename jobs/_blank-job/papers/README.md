# Papers for this job

This job's papers go here: the estimate, the contract, plans, permits, inspection reports, supplier quotes, change orders, photos and anything else that matters to the job.

- **Name each one with the date first,** then what it is, in lowercase with dashes: `2026-03-14-signed-contract.pdf`, `2026-04-02-rough-electrical-inspection.pdf`.
- **Every paper gets one line in the job record,** in its Papers table: what it is, and what it means for the job.
- **Never put these here:** tax forms, anything with a Social Security number, tax ID or license number, bank or card details, ID copies, passwords, insurance or bonding papers with policy numbers, or pay records. Write the fact in the job record instead.
