# How jobs work

Every job gets its own folder here, from the first call to the closeout. Each new job starts as a copy of the [blank job](_blank-job/job-record.md). Your assistant makes the copy when you say "New lead."

## Stages

Every job moves through these, in order:

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| Lead | Someone called or wrote. We haven't priced it yet. |
| Estimate | We're pricing it, or the estimate is out with the customer. |
| Contract | The customer said yes. Contract and deposit are in hand or on the way. |
| Scheduled | It has a start date. Materials and subs are being lined up. |
| In progress | We're on site. |
| Closeout | The work is done. Punch list, final payment and the closeout write-up. |
| Done | Paid, closed out, lessons written down. |
| Lost | It didn't go ahead. One line on why. Lost leads are kept. |

## The closeout loop

A job isn't Done until its closeout is written: what we estimated against what it really cost and took, what went well, what didn't, and what we'd do differently. The lessons go on the [lessons-learned page](../lessons/lessons-learned.md), and, if you agree, into the standard or policy they affect. Your assistant reads those lessons and past closeouts before every new estimate. That's how each job makes the next one better.

## Papers

A job's papers (estimate, contract, plans, permits, inspection reports, quotes, change orders, photos) go in that job's papers folder. See the [papers page in the blank job](_blank-job/papers/README.md).
