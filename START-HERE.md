# Start here

This folder is how your business runs, written down: what the company is, how you do the work, how you price and get paid, every job from the first call to the closeout, who you buy from and who you sub out to, and what you've learned along the way. Your AI assistant reads it at the start of every session and keeps it current as you talk.

If you haven't set up your computer yet, do the [setup guide](SETUP.md) first.

## Start every session with these lines

Open the Claude app, click **`</>`** at the top left, start a new conversation, check the folder under the message box is your records folder, and paste:

```text
We're starting a working session in my business-records folder. Before anything else, read the instruction file at the top of this folder and follow it, including bringing the folder up to date. If you can't read the folder or can't bring it up to date, say so plainly in one sentence and stop. Don't carry on with what you have, and don't offer to work from a copy I upload. If you find unsaved work already sitting there, tell me what it is in one sentence and wait for me. As we work, save each time something I've confirmed is written down, and tell me "Saved." When you're ready, tell me in one line.
```

When you stop for the day, if the last reply doesn't end with "Saved.", say "Save that."

## Things you can say

You can talk to your assistant in your own words. These phrases have a fixed meaning:

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| Help me get started | It checks everything works, then walks you through the first hour below |
| New lead | It starts a new job and asks you about it, a few questions at a time |
| Where are we on (job) | Stage, next step, what it's waiting on, and anything owed |
| What's open | Every job that isn't finished or lost, in a few lines |
| Save that | Saves right now |
| Undo that | Takes back the last save |
| Help me give someone access | Walks you through letting someone see or work on your records |
| Take someone's access away | Walks you through removing them |

## Your first hour

Do these in order. Your assistant asks the questions and writes the answers down. You don't need to open any of these pages yourself.

1. **One live job.** Pick a job you're running now and tell your assistant where it stands. Hand it the estimate, contract and notes you have for it, never tax forms, bank details or ID copies. Then ask it something you'd normally answer from memory.
2. **The company pages.** What the company is, what you do and don't do, who works with you, and where you work. About fifteen minutes.
3. **The policies.** How you build a price, your payment schedule, how change orders work, and your warranty. Use your own numbers. Where you haven't decided something, leave it blank; your assistant won't fill it in for you.
4. **One past job, with its closeout.** Pick a finished job you remember well. What did you estimate, what did it really cost, what went wrong, what would you do differently? This is the part that makes the next estimate better.

There are two invented sample jobs in the jobs folder, a finished kitchen remodel and a deck build in progress, so you can see what a filled-in job looks like. Once you have a few real jobs, ask your assistant to delete them.

## Keep in mind

- **Your records must stay private on GitHub.** Your assistant checks this every session and stops saving if they're ever public.
- **Never hand your assistant** tax forms, bank or card details, ID copies, passwords, insurance or bonding papers with policy numbers, or pay records. Tell it the fact instead. The [setup guide](SETUP.md#papers-you-never-hand-your-assistant) has the full list.
- **Read from your phone, change from your computer.**
