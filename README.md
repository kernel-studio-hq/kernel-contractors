A free, open-source business brain for contractors. Organize your company knowledge, workflows, and operating standards into a structured foundation that gives AI the context behind how your business runs.

Your estimates, jobs, policies and lessons, written down once so your AI assistant can work from them every day.

## What it is

A set of plain pages that describe how a small contracting business runs: the company basics, your standards and policies, every job from first call to closeout, your suppliers and subs, and the lessons you learn across jobs. It is written for businesses of two to fifteen people, such as remodelers, tile and stone, carpentry, cabinetry, decks and design-build. You copy it into your own private GitHub repository and fill it in with your AI assistant. After that, your assistant reads it at the start of every session and keeps it current as you work.

## What it costs to run

The template is free. Running it is not. You need:

- **your own paid AI assistant plan.** The setup guide is written for a paid Claude plan, using the Claude desktop app in its Code tab. The free plan cannot work in a folder on your computer.
- **your own GitHub account.** A basic account is free, and private repositories are included.

Other assistants that read a file called `AGENTS.md` may work too, but the setup guide doesn't cover them.

## Two ways to use it

**Set it up yourself.** Follow the [setup guide](SETUP.md). Allow about an hour. It takes some comfort with a terminal window: you'll install a sign-in tool, paste commands one at a time and check what each one prints. If that sounds like a bad afternoon, the second way is for you.

**Have it set up for you.** Most owners would rather not spend an afternoon in a terminal window. Kernel Studio can do the setup with you: getting your computer ready, making your private copy, and filling in the company pages, your policies, one live job and one past job together. We can also keep it current as new versions come out. To ask about it, email support@onekernel.io.

Either way, your copy must be a **private** repository. It will hold customer names, addresses, contracts and prices.

## What's inside

| Page or folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md) | The owner's page: how to start each session, what to say, and the first hour |
| [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) | Step-by-step setup on your own computer |
| [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) | Standing instructions your assistant reads every session (two identical copies, for different assistants) |
| [company/](company/) | About the company, services, team, service area |
| [standards/](standards/) | Quality, customer communication, job site rules |
| [policies/](policies/) | Estimating, payments and deposits, change orders, warranty |
| [jobs/](jobs/) | One folder per job, a blank job to copy, and two invented samples |
| [suppliers-and-subs/](suppliers-and-subs/) | Suppliers and subcontractors |
| [lessons/](lessons/) | Lessons learned across jobs |

The company, standards, policies, suppliers and lessons pages are blank templates. They hold the structure and leave the numbers to you. Nothing in them is pricing advice.
