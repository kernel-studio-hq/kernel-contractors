A free, open-source business brain for contractors. Organize your company knowledge, workflows, and operating standards into a structured foundation that gives AI the context behind how your business runs.

## Stop running your business from memory

What did we promise the homeowner? Which permits do we need to check before we start? Who did we use for the last job like this? How do we handle it when the scope changes?

When the answers live in your head, old texts, and job folders, every question ends up back on your plate.

**Kernel for Contractors is a free GitHub template for building an AI workspace that knows how your business runs.** The template is free to copy; running it takes your own paid AI assistant plan and a GitHub account. Give your assistant your job history, standards, policies, and lessons so it can help you find answers and get work done the way your company does it.

- **Before work starts:** Pull up a similar job, draft a scope, and check what permits and inspections similar jobs needed, so you know what to confirm before you start.
- **While work is underway:** Find what you promised the customer, draft a change order using your policy, or pull up the sub you used last time.
- **After the job wraps:** Capture what cost you time or money and turn it into a lesson, or a standard the next crew follows.

Start with one live job. Give your assistant the estimates, contracts, and job notes you already have (never tax forms, bank details, or ID copies) and ask it a question you'd normally have to answer from memory.

## Who it's for

Built for contractors and trade businesses that want one place to keep track of how they run jobs and how they run the company. Create your own private copy on GitHub and start with whatever you reach for most often: job records, permit checklists, crew standards, or company policies. Your AI assistant uses those records as you work and can help you update them as the business changes.

## What it costs to run

The template is free. Running it is not. You need:

- **your own paid AI assistant plan.** The setup guide is written for a paid <a href="https://claude.com/product/overview" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Claude plan</a>, using the Claude desktop app in its Code tab. The free plan cannot work in a folder on your computer.
- **your own GitHub account.** A basic account is free, and private repositories are included.

Other assistants that read a file called `AGENTS.md` may work too, but the setup guide doesn't cover them.

## Two ways to use it

**Set it up yourself.** Follow the [setup guide](SETUP.md). Allow about an hour. It takes some comfort with a terminal window: you'll install a sign-in tool, paste commands one at a time and check what each one prints. If that sounds like a bad afternoon, the second way is for you.

**Have it set up for you.** Most owners would rather not spend an afternoon in a terminal window. Kernel Studio can do the setup with you: getting your computer ready, making your private copy, and filling in one live job, the company pages, your policies and one past job together. We can also keep it current as new versions come out. To ask about it, email support@onekernel.io.

Either way, your copy must be a **private** repository. It will hold customer names, addresses, contracts and prices.

## What's inside

| Page or folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md) | The owner's page: how to start each session, what to say, and the first hour |
| [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) | Step-by-step setup on your own computer |
| [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) | Standing instructions your assistant reads every session (two identical copies, for different assistants) |
| [company/](company/) | About the company, services, team, service area |
| [standards/](standards/) | Quality, customer communication, job site rules |
| [policies/](policies/) | Estimating, payments and deposits, change orders, warranty |
| [jobs/](jobs/) | One folder per job, a blank job to copy, and two invented samples |
| [suppliers-and-subs/](suppliers-and-subs/) | Suppliers and subcontractors |
| [lessons/](lessons/) | Lessons learned across jobs |

The company, standards, policies, suppliers and lessons pages are blank templates. They hold the structure and leave the numbers to you. Nothing in them is pricing advice.
